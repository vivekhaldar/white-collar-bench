Tue 2 Mar, 10:00-12:30, Registrar's Office, Saltmarren Admin Bldg (3rd fl). Transfer evaluation discovery.
Who: me, Delphine (interim registrar), Rufus (transfer credit coordinator), + Tillie from advising for the last half hour.

## evaluators

- site visit done. interviews w/ 3 transfer evaluators, ~25 min each, Rufus sat in on all three. no records copied, nothing left campus (per Aga)
- walked Rufus's process map w/ them - matches what they actually do, give or take
- time sink = step 6, course-by-course equivalency lookup. every evaluator said it unprompted

  - another said a 2-transcript student is ~1 hr, a 4-transcript student "is my afternoon"

- they'd take help on the lookup / suggested matches, NOT on the final call. fine, that's the scope anyway
