Who is right? The rules disagree

A benchmark bug, found by a model that read the rules literally
The memory contract

The world generator
A direct request is accepted
Share of new loops a model marks unaccepted: Sol 24% · Haiku 21% · Opus 15% · Sonnet 14%. Fix: unanswered asks are optional, neither required nor wrong.

Takeaways

  1. Lite works. Same ranking as full runs at ~20% of the cost.
  2. Opus leads, Haiku is the bargain. 89 on Track A for $3.53.
  3. Own memory is the real test. Track B separates models.
